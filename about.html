Our Story

We started because local businesses deserved better.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Radical Honesty

We tell you what your project actually needs — not what inflates the invoice. If a simpler solution works, we'll say so.

Bias Toward Action

We move fast and ship real things. Progress beats planning every time — and if something breaks, we fix it, fast.

Long-Term Partnership

We're not here to deliver a website and disappear. We're here to grow alongside your business, year after year.

How We Got Here

From a single project
to a full team.

We didn't start with a business plan. We started with a broken website and a client who needed help. Everything since has been built on that same instinct: show up, solve the problem, stay.

Year One

The First Client

A local retailer needed their website rebuilt after it was hacked. We delivered in two weeks — and they're still a client today.

Growth Phase

Expanding Services

Word spread. We added custom software, cybersecurity, and IT support to keep up with what clients actually needed — not just what we wanted to sell.

Finding Our Focus

Local-First Positioning

We made a deliberate decision to stay focused on local and regional businesses rather than chasing enterprise contracts. That's where we do our best work.

Today

Still Showing Up

Same commitment, wider capability. We're a small, sharp team with the range to take on anything from a landing page to a full platform rebuild.

Our Approach

We build differently — and it shows.

Documentation That Actually Exists

We document everything — architecture decisions, deployment steps, credentials. When you need to hand off or hire internally, you're not starting from zero.

You Always Know What's Happening

Weekly check-ins, transparent project boards, and a direct line to your developer — not a ticketing system that eats your messages.

We Measure What Matters

Deliverables are a means to an end. We track business outcomes — leads, conversions, time saved — not just GitHub commits.

Security Is Not Optional

We bake security in from the first line of code — not as a post-launch checkbox, but as a core design principle.
