Common Questions

Before you reach out

How much does a website cost?

It depends on scope, but most small business websites start around $1,500. Custom software and platform projects are priced per-project after a free discovery call.

How long does a project take?

A standard website takes 2–4 weeks. Larger software projects are scoped individually, but we set realistic timelines from day one — no moving goalposts.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes. We offer monthly care plans that cover updates, security patches, uptime monitoring, and priority support. No long contracts required.

We already have a website — can you improve it?

Absolutely. Whether it's a redesign, a performance fix, or adding new features, we work with existing sites all the time. Send us the URL and we'll take a look.
